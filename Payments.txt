🔹 1️⃣ Basic Payment Flow (Very Important)

In interviews, first explain the end-to-end flow:

💳 Example: Online Payment (Card Payment)

User selects product

Clicks Pay

Enters card details

Merchant sends request to Payment Gateway

Gateway sends to Acquiring Bank

Acquiring bank contacts Card Network (Visa/Mastercard)

Card network contacts Issuing Bank

Issuing bank approves/declines

Response flows back

Transaction status updated (Success/Failure)
